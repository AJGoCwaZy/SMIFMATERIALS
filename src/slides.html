How the plant works
  1. Compress. Electric motors squeeze outside air. Power is the largest running cost.
  2. Clean and cool. Adsorbers strip water and CO2, then the air is chilled until it turns liquid.
  3. Split. In the cold box each gas boils off at its own temperature: nitrogen, argon, oxygen.
